## What I Do

I assess rollout posture, rollback options, observability, and unresolved release risk for medium and high-risk changes.

## When To Use Me

- when a change is medium or high risk
- when contracts, migrations, or deployment behavior changed
- when review or planning needs explicit release safeguards

- rollout considerations
- rollback posture
- observability or smoke-check signal
- unresolved release risks
